# A semantic view change only breaks answers you notice

**Claim (paraphrased):** editing a semantic view definition is safe to merge if it still deploys; broken answers show up later, in front of users.

**What was tested:** `check.sh` pins the scope first: the questions of `seeds/question_pack.csv` whose metric the **main** branch's `snowflake/semantic_view.sql` defines (Q01-Q06). It then parses the METRICS block of the view in the tree under test (the parsed entry count must equal an independent count of `AS` heads and `COMMENT`s), recomputes every pinned question from those expressions over the frozen seeds on DuckDB, and compares each with the owner's frozen `expected_answer` (within `tolerance`; UNDEFINED must stay undefined). A pinned question whose metric was renamed or removed is a FAIL, not a skip. Two planted edits must each go RED on exactly the answers they break: `conversion_rate` divided by `active_users` instead of `session_count` (Q04, Q05 only), and `conversion_rate` coalesced to 0 when there are no sessions (Q06 only). A dbt or parser failure is a FAIL, never counted as the required RED. No Snowflake call, no spend. It runs on every PR through `proofs/run_all.sh` in `scripts/pre-pr.sh`.

**Result:** current definition: 6 of 6 answers match. Denominator edit: Q04 moves 0.0809 -> 0.3389 and Q05 0.0879 -> 0.2489, nothing else. Zero-coalesce edit: Q06 moves UNDEFINED -> 0.0, nothing else. `NUMBERS: in_scope=6 clean_ok=6 mutA_moved=Q04,Q05 mutB_moved=Q06`.

**Limit:** only the metric expressions are read from the view. The anchoring (a base metric filters its own table on its own date column; a derived metric anchors bookings to the sessions in the slice) and the date and channel filters are fixed in `svsql.py`, not read from the view, so an edit to the view's relationships, facts or filters is not tested. The gate replays the view's arithmetic, not Cortex Analyst's SQL generation; questions whose metric is not in the view (Q07-Q10) are out of its scope.
